Fab Academy & Aalto FabLab 2026 · ONGOING

OmniPen — a tangible AI interface prototype

Rapid prototyping · Digital Fabrication

A pen-like wearable device exploring possibilities of an AI embedded in a physical, haptic tool rather than on a screen. Built from scratch — PCB design in KiCad, embedded firmware, 3D-printed housing. Its incomplete (at least for now), but it's a working research prototype and a statement about HTI should go. The design choices are intentional: no screen, no cloud dependency, no account required, AI as a quiet assistant in the background rather than a dominant interface.

New Media Design · Aalto 2021

EmotiGo — a wearable badge for low-threshold social signalling

Solo design project · Concept design

A physical badge that lets you communicate silent intentions/emotional availability in public spaces — without a phone. You toggle the badge to set your emoji, say "open" or "close" into it, and use a built-in laser to send micro-messages to other badges nearby. Deliberately offline: no internet, no data, no social scoring. The design started from a simple observation — that people constantly assume what others are thinking but rarely ask — and turned into a design concept stating how tangible interfaces can lower the threshold for authentic connection.
