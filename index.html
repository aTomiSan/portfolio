01 / Fab Academy 2025–2026

OmniPen — a tangible AI interface prototype [ONGOING]

Physical computing · Embedded systems · AI integration · Aalto FabLab

A pen-like wearable device exploring possibilities of an AI embedded in a physical, haptic tool rather than on a screen. Built from scratch — PCB design in KiCad, embedded firmware, 3D-printed housing. Its incomplete (at least for now), but it's a working research prototype and a statement about HTI should go. The design choices are intentional: no screen, no cloud dependency, no account required, AI as a quiet assistant in the background rather than a dominant interface.

03 / Aalto ITP · Finnish Ministry of Education 2021 · 3 months

User research & web service for digitalisation of Finnish basic education

Team Snowbird · Creative leadership · UX research · Service design

Commissioned by the Finnish Ministry of Education and Culture. As creative lead, ran a full user research cycle across teachers, Digitutors, and school and municipal administration. The core finding: information flows only top-down in the national digitalisation framework, and teachers are left to figure things out in isolation. We designed a prototype knowledge-sharing platform — peer Q&A, resource bank, notification system — and presented it at Demo Day. The deck is below.

04 / New Media Design · Aalto 2021

EmotiGo — a wearable badge for low-threshold social signalling

Solo design project · Tangible interaction · Social computing

A physical badge that lets you communicate emotional availability in public spaces — without a phone. You twist the badge to set your emoji, say "open" or "close" into it, and use a built-in laser to send micro-messages to other badges nearby. Deliberately offline: no internet, no data, no social scoring. The design started from a simple observation — that people constantly assume what others are thinking but rarely ask — and turned into a research question about how tangible interfaces can lower the threshold for authentic connection.
